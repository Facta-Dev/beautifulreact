# /grilling

## Delegate

Delegate to the **grilling** skill.

Activate when an `<app>` plan, decision or idea needs to be stress-tested
before anyone implements it: the scope is fuzzy, the assumptions are
unwritten, the failure modes are unnamed, or the requester asks to be
challenged. The skill maps the plan as a design tree and works it in rounds,
asking the whole ready frontier at once, each question with a recommended
answer, until nothing is left silently assumed.

Do not activate for an agreed plan that is ready to execute, for a factual
lookup, or for a diff review (use `review`).
